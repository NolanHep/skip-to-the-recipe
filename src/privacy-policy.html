Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13 February 2025

Welcome to Skip to the Recipe! This policy is here to explain, in plain language, how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our services. By using our site, you're agreeing to the practices described here.

What Information Do We Collect?

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website or make API requests, we automatically gather a few pieces of information to help us keep things running smoothly:

  • Visits and API Requests: We count the number visit to our website and the number of API requests made. This helps us understand how our site is used and where we can make improvements.
  • Referral Data: Each time you visit or use our API, your browser sends us the URL of the site that referred you here. We use this information only to add to our referral counter, which helps us with analytics and operational insights.

How Do We Use This Information?

We use the data we collect in a couple of important ways:

  • Improving Performance: By looking at referral data and request counts, we can better understand how our website and API are performing. This means we can work on making your experience smoother and more enjoyable.
  • Keeping Things Running: We monitor these counts to ensure our website stays healthy, manage traffic effectively, and maintain the security and reliability of our services.

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

We only keep the collected data such as visit counts, API request counts, and referral data for as long as it's needed to serve the purposes mentioned above or as required by law.

Any information submitted to our website is securely sent to its destination.

How Do We Protect Your Data?

Your privacy is important to us. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect the information we collect. That said, no security system is perfect, and we cannot guarantee complete security.

Will We Share Your Information?

We take your privacy seriously and do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties—unless:

In the occasion that the law requires us, or if a law enforcement agency makes a valid request, we may need to share your information.

What About Links to Other Sites?

Our website might include links to other sites that we don't control. This policy doesn't cover those sites, so please check their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

We are not responsible for anything that happens on linked sites and are not affiliated with them.

Can This Policy Change?

Yes, we may update our privacy policy from time to time. If changes are made, we'll post the new version on this page with an updated effective date. We recommend checking this page periodically so you're always aware of how we're protecting your information.
